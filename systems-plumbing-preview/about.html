About Systems Plumbing

COLORADO BUILT.
RELATIONSHIP DRIVEN.

Bruce Kraynak and Duane Terry started Systems Plumbing in 2001 after many years of plumbing installation field experience. Today, they lead one of the most respected and sought-after plumbing installation companies among builders of new residential homes in Colorado.

Leadership

The company’s story, carried by experienced leadership.

Bruce Kraynak

Co-Founder

Bruce is a Pittsburgh native who moved to Colorado in 1979. He learned his craft from the ground up, progressing from apprentice to master plumber. His career began in the solar energy field before moving into the residential plumbing industry.

Proficient as a field plumber and foreman, Bruce successfully transitioned into project management, general management, and company leadership through extensive real-life experience.

His business development has been enhanced through involvement with peer organizations such as Vistage and service on numerous industry and community nonprofits. He currently serves as president of the Colorado chapter of Plumbing Heating and Cooling Contractors (PHCC) board of trustees, giving back to the industry through trade development, legislative issues, and code improvement.

Duane Terry

Co-Founder & CFO

Duane is a Colorado native who transitioned into plumbing after early entrepreneurial ventures and has worked in the plumbing field for more than 30 years.

He is a highly competent owner and CFO with experience and expertise across the financial, technical, and administrative aspects of company leadership.

Together, Bruce and Duane have helped shape Systems Plumbing into a company known for reliability, professionalism, and high-quality residential installation work.

Residential plumbing installation specialists serving the Denver Front Range from Ft. Collins to Castle Rock. Systems Plumbing installs plumbing in new single family homes, town homes, and condominiums.
